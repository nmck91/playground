# Project Brief: Football Director

**Date:** 2025-12-17
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft

---

## Executive Summary

**Football Director** is a web and mobile football club management simulation game where players take on the role of a club chairman/director. Unlike tactical management games that focus on match-day decisions, Football Director emphasizes the business, strategic, and administrative aspects of running a football club. Players will navigate transfer markets, manage finances, develop infrastructure, hire staff, and make critical decisions that determine their club's long-term success.

**Target Market:** Football/soccer fans aged 18-45 who enjoy strategic simulation games, particularly those who appreciate the business side of sports.

**Key Value Proposition:** An accessible, engaging simulation that captures the strategic depth of football club management without requiring tactical expertise, playable anywhere on web or mobile devices.

---

## Problem Statement

### Current State & Pain Points

Football management games have traditionally focused on tactical match-day gameplay (e.g., Football Manager), which can be:
- **Time-intensive:** Requiring hours of gameplay sessions
- **Complex:** Steep learning curves with overwhelming tactical options
- **Desktop-bound:** Limited mobile accessibility for casual play
- **Tactically focused:** Requiring football knowledge about formations, tactics, and match strategies

### The Gap in the Market

There's a underserved segment of football fans who:
- Love the sport and want to engage with it strategically
- Prefer business/management simulation over tactical gameplay
- Want shorter, more casual play sessions
- Desire cross-platform accessibility (play on commute via mobile, continue at home on web)
- Are interested in the "chairman experience" rather than the "manager experience"

### Why Now?

1. **Mobile gaming growth:** 60%+ of gaming happens on mobile devices
2. **Incremental game success:** Proven model for casual, session-based gameplay
3. **Sports management popularity:** Fantasy football and sports betting show strong interest in strategic sports decision-making
4. **Web technology maturity:** Modern frameworks enable seamless web/mobile experiences

## Proposed Solution

### Core Concept

Football Director delivers a streamlined, accessible football club management experience focused on high-level strategic and business decisions. Players experience the thrill of building a club from modest beginnings to elite status through smart decision-making in:

- **Transfer market strategy:** Scouting, negotiating, and building a competitive squad within budget constraints
- **Financial management:** Balancing budgets, securing sponsorships, managing ticket pricing
- **Infrastructure development:** Stadium expansions, training facilities, youth academies
- **Staff management:** Hiring managers, coaches, scouts, and administrative staff
- **Board relations:** Meeting objectives, managing expectations, maintaining job security

### Key Differentiators

1. **Accessibility First:** Simple, intuitive interface focused on meaningful choices, not overwhelming data
2. **Session-Based Gameplay:** Designed for 5-15 minute play sessions that feel productive
3. **Business Focus:** Strategic chairman-level decisions, not tactical match-day management
4. **Cross-Platform Continuity:** Seamless experience across web and mobile with cloud saves
5. **Progression Satisfaction:** Clear advancement mechanics and visible impact of decisions

### Why This Will Succeed

- **Proven concept:** Football Chairman Pro has demonstrated sustained player engagement
- **Modern platform:** Web/mobile accessibility removes barriers to entry
- **Unique positioning:** Fills gap between complex tactical sims and casual mobile games
- **Retention mechanics:** Natural progression loops and season-based gameplay create sustained engagement

## MVP Scope

### Core Features (Must Have)

- **Club Management Dashboard:** Central hub showing club status, finances, upcoming fixtures, league table, and key alerts
  - *Rationale: Core interface for all player interaction*

- **League Simulation System:** Automated match simulation with realistic results based on squad strength, with basic match summaries
  - *Rationale: Foundation of gameplay loop - matches drive everything else*

- **Transfer Market:** Browse available players, make offers, negotiate contracts, manage squad
  - *Rationale: Most engaging strategic decision-making*

- **Financial Management:** Budget tracking, income/expenses, basic sponsorship deals, wage management
  - *Rationale: Creates meaningful constraints and strategic trade-offs*

- **Staff Management:** Hire/fire manager and coaches with different skill levels affecting team performance
  - *Rationale: Adds strategic depth and another lever for improving performance*

- **Stadium Development:** Upgrade stadium capacity to increase revenue
  - *Rationale: Long-term progression goal and revenue driver*

- **Board Objectives:** Season targets (league position, financial goals) with job security consequences
  - *Rationale: Creates tension, goals, and stakes*

- **Career Progression:** Start at lower division club, ability to be approached by bigger clubs based on success
  - *Rationale: Long-term meta-progression to keep players engaged*

- **Cloud Save System:** Account creation and game state persistence across devices
  - *Rationale: Essential for cross-platform experience*

- **Responsive UI:** Functional experience on mobile and desktop
  - *Rationale: Core value proposition*

### Out of Scope for MVP

- Multiplayer or competitive features
- Tactical match control or detailed match engine
- Youth academy system
- International management
- Historical clubs or real player names (use generated names/clubs initially)
- Advanced analytics and detailed statistics
- Achievements/trophy system
- Social features or sharing
- Monetization features (ads, IAP, subscriptions)
- Multiple save slots
- Custom club creation

### MVP Success Criteria

**MVP is successful if:**
1. Core gameplay loop (matches → results → decisions → progression) is engaging for 5+ seasons
2. Players understand and engage with transfer market and financial management
3. Technical performance meets KPI targets (<2s load, <1% error rate)
4. 40%+ of beta testers return for Day 7 session
5. Positive qualitative feedback on "chairman experience" feel and accessibility

## Technical Considerations

### Platform Requirements

- **Target Platforms:** Web (desktop and mobile browsers), Progressive Web App (PWA) for mobile install
- **Browser/OS Support:**
  - Modern browsers: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge (last 2 versions)
  - Mobile: iOS 14+, Android 9+
- **Performance Requirements:**
  - Initial load: <2 seconds
  - Route transitions: <500ms
  - Match simulation: <1 second per match
  - Responsive at 60fps on mid-range mobile devices (2019+)

### Technology Preferences

**Frontend:**
- **Framework:** **Next.js** (DECIDED - first React project in the monorepo!)
- **Rationale:**
  - Validates polyglot monorepo strategy
  - Faster iteration with AI-assisted development
  - Better ecosystem for game UIs and state-heavy apps
  - SSR/SSG capabilities for marketing pages
- **State Management:** Zustand or React Context for game state management
- **UI Framework:** **Tailwind CSS using existing `libs/tailwind-preset`**
- **Design System:** Maintain consistency with existing apps (family-calendar, math-quest, reward-chart)
  - Reuse design tokens and component patterns
  - Create `libs/football-director-ui` for game-specific components
  - Follow established styling conventions
- **Testing:** **100% unit test coverage required**
  - Jest for unit/integration tests (already configured in monorepo)
  - React Testing Library for component tests
  - Vitest as alternative (faster, better DX)
  - All game logic must be tested (simulation engine, calculations, state management)

**Backend:**
- **API:** Node.js with Express or NestJS (fits Nx monorepo well)
- **Game Logic:** TypeScript shared library between frontend/backend
- **Authentication:** Supabase Auth (already in use per package.json) or similar
- **Real-time:** WebSockets for live notifications (future feature)

**Database:**
- **Primary:** PostgreSQL via Supabase (already integrated)
- **Structure:** Player data, club data, save states, user accounts
- **Caching:** Redis for session data and frequently accessed game data (future optimization)

**Hosting/Infrastructure:**
- **Frontend:** Vercel (optimal for Next.js) or similar edge hosting
- **Backend:** Cloud-hosted API (Railway, Render, or AWS Lambda for serverless)
- **Database:** Supabase hosted PostgreSQL
- **CDN:** Cloudflare or Vercel Edge for global performance

### Architecture Considerations

**Repository Structure:**
- Leverage existing Nx monorepo structure
- Create new app: `apps/football-director`
- Shared game engine library: `libs/football-director-engine` (game logic, simulation)
- Shared UI components: Potentially extend existing `libs/tailwind-preset` or create `libs/football-director-ui`
- API package: `apps/football-director-api` (if needed for multiplayer/cloud features)

**Service Architecture:**
- MVP: Monolithic frontend with game logic in-browser
- Client-side game state with cloud backup/restore
- Future: Backend API for validation, multiplayer, anti-cheat

**Integration Requirements:**
- Supabase for auth and database (already integrated in monorepo)
- Analytics platform (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar)
- Error tracking (Sentry)
- Cloud storage for game saves

**Security/Compliance:**
- Secure authentication flow
- Client-side game state encryption for anti-tampering
- GDPR compliance for EU users (data export, deletion)
- Rate limiting on API endpoints
- Input validation to prevent exploits

## Risks & Open Questions

### Key Risks

- **Player Engagement:** Core gameplay loop may not be compelling enough to retain users past initial novelty
  - *Mitigation: Early beta testing with target users, rapid iteration on feedback*

- **Technical Performance:** Client-side simulation of full season may cause performance issues on lower-end mobile devices
  - *Mitigation: Performance profiling, optimization, potential backend simulation for slower devices*

- **Market Competition:** Existing games (Football Chairman Pro, other management sims) may dominate search/discovery
  - *Mitigation: Differentiate on platform accessibility, modern UX, free-to-play model*

- **Monetization Uncertainty:** Unclear what monetization model will work best without compromising UX
  - *Mitigation: Launch free MVP, gather data on user behavior and willingness to pay*

- **Scope Creep:** Feature-rich vision may lead to delayed MVP or bloated initial release
  - *Mitigation: Strict MVP discipline, ruthless prioritization, phased rollout plan*

### Open Questions

- Should MVP include real club names/leagues, or generic equivalents? (Licensing complexity vs. immersion)
- What's the optimal balance between realism and accessibility in simulation logic?
- How much data/statistics should be exposed to players without overwhelming them?
- Should match results be fully deterministic (replayable) or have random elements?
- What authentication method will provide lowest friction for user onboarding?
- Is PWA sufficient for mobile, or should native apps be prioritized sooner?

### Areas Needing Further Research

- **Competitive Analysis:** Deep dive into Football Chairman Pro's current state, pricing, features, user reviews
- **User Validation:** Survey/interview target users to validate problem statement and solution approach
- **Technical Feasibility:** Prototype match simulation performance on target mobile devices
- **Game Balance:** Research sports sim game balance (squad strength calculation, financial models)
- **Monetization Models:** Research successful monetization in similar casual management games
- **Discovery Strategy:** How do players find and try games like this? (App stores, web search, communities)
